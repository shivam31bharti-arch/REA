# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Electron + React (user's choice). TypeScript and Vite are delegated choices: typed components across four apps, and a fast dev server for iterating on the renderer.

## Users

- **The creator:** builds Paperlight on camera for a YouTube channel, and uses it daily on Windows for real documents, spreadsheets, presentations and notes.
- **Viewers:** watch the build and the finished suite in screen recordings. They judge it on whether it looks and works as well as the paid suite it replaces.

## Product Purpose

Paperlight is a free, local office suite written from scratch: four original apps that do the everyday jobs of a word processor, spreadsheet, presentation tool and notebook. It has no subscription, no account and no cloud; files stay on the computer. Success means about 70–85% of everyday tasks work, and the design is visibly more refined than Microsoft Office on screen.

## Positioning

An original suite, not a copy. It interoperates through open document standards (`.docx`, `.xlsx`, `.pptx`) but shares no code, icons, templates, fonts or look with Microsoft Office. All four apps share one design system and interaction model, so the suite reads as one product.

## Operating Context

- Runs as a desktop app on Windows 11 (build 22000).
- Recorded with screen capture for YouTube, so screens must read clearly when compressed and viewed on a phone.
- Daily use for real work, including exchanging files with people who use Microsoft Office.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Apps and the jobs they replace:

| App | Replaces | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Prose | Word | `.docx` open and save, PDF export |
| Cells | Excel | `.xlsx` |
| Deck | PowerPoint | `.pptx` export, PDF |
| Jotbook | OneNote | Local notebooks |

- Build order: suite shell and home screen, then Prose as the design proof, then Jotbook, Cells and Deck.
- No Microsoft code, icons, templates, fonts or branding. Names must not imitate Microsoft trademarks.
- Local only: no accounts, network services or telemetry.
- Open-source dependencies only, with licenses compatible with free distribution.
- Undecided: whether viewers will be offered an installer. Not a current goal.

## Brand Commitments

- Suite name: **Paperlight**.
- App names: **Prose** (documents), **Cells** (spreadsheets), **Deck** (presentations), **Jotbook** (notes). Chosen by the user.

## Evidence on Hand

None. There are no users, testimonials, download counts or benchmarks. Demo documents and sample content are synthetic and must be labeled or obviously illustrative; no claims about adoption or performance may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. **Local and free, always.** Nothing requires an account, a network or payment.
2. **Everyday jobs first.** Cover the common tasks well before chasing rare features.
3. **One suite, one hand.** Four apps, one design language and one way of working.
4. **Interoperate, don't imitate.** Speak Office's file formats; never borrow its look.
5. **Camera-ready.** Every screen must read clearly in a screen recording.
