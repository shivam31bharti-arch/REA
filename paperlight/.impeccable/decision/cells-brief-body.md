## Scope

Cells, the spreadsheet app: workbooks of sheets on a fast grid with formulas, cell formatting, sort and filter, and charts; it opens and saves real .xlsx files. Mode: Operate. A whole surface inside the established Paperlight world (DESIGN.md); the world is fixed and only the composition was chosen.

Audience: the creator tracking channel numbers, budgets and plans daily, exchanging .xlsx files with people who use Excel, and viewers of the recording. Task: enter numbers and formulas, read totals, sort and filter, chart a range, save a file Excel opens. Constraint: nothing leaves the computer, and the screen must read clearly in a compressed recording.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The workbook's sheets are numbered down a strip beside the grid, each with a row-count length bar, the way Jotbook numbers pages and Prose numbers sections. It refuses the category default of tiny tabs along the bottom edge under a ribbon.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited unchanged from DESIGN.md. Cobalt #2448D8 fills the rail and is the accent: the current sheet numeral, the active cell outline, the selected range tint, the selection's header highlight, the first chart series. Cobalt as text is #2448D8 on light and #7D97FF on dark. White ground, #111 ink, 1px hairlines, square corners, no shadows, Geist with tabular figures in the grid, Geist Mono only for the cell address, the formula and the stamp.

STORY: The viewer sees a calm workbook where the grid owns the screen. The user picks a sheet from the numbered strip, types numbers and =formulas into cells or the formula line, formats with B/I/U and number formats, sorts and filters from the bar, drags across a range to read Sum, Average and Count in the status bar, inserts a chart from the selection, and saves a real .xlsx: the stamp shows Saved with the time.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 64px cobalt rail. 56px command bar: "Cells /" and the workbook title, a Number format menu, B/I/U, fill, alignment, Sort, Filter, Chart, Insert, the Ctrl K search pill, the stamp and a filled "Save". A 44px formula line over the grid: a mono address box and the active cell's formula. A 240px sheet strip of 42px/800 numerals with sheet names, row-count bars and "New sheet". The grid fills the rest: 30px rows, sticky column letters and row numbers, the active cell outlined 2px in cobalt. A 32px status bar: sheet position, selection figures, file state.

FORM: Sheet strip, #3 on the ordered structural list for this surface. seed: 3f4b3b59f0ce431c. Signature interaction: the numbered sheet strip, live: the current sheet's numeral in cobalt and each sheet's bar growing as rows are filled; the selection's figures update in the status bar as the range is dragged. Motion moves on one axis, 160 to 200ms, ease-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
