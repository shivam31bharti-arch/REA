## Scope

Jotbook, the notebook app: notebooks > sections > pages of rich notes with checklists, images and search, autosaved locally. Mode: Operate. A whole surface inside the established Paperlight world (DESIGN.md); the world is fixed and only the composition was chosen.

Audience: the creator keeping ideas, shot lists and meeting notes daily, and viewers of the recording. Task: capture a note fast, find it again later, tick things off. Constraint: nothing leaves the computer, and the screen must read clearly in a compressed recording.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One wide note with its section's pages numbered beside it, the way Prose numbers its sections. It refuses the category default of three always-open panes that squeeze the note into a narrow column.

OWN-WORLD: Inherited unchanged from DESIGN.md. Viridian #0B7A6C fills the rail and is the accent: the current page numeral, the caret, selection, checked tasks, the selected palette and "/" items. Viridian as text is #0B7A6C on light and #3FBFA9 on dark. White ground, #111 ink, 1px hairlines, square corners, no shadows, Geist with Geist Mono only for figures and the stamp.

STORY: The viewer sees a calm notebook where the note owns the screen. The writer switches notebook and section from the bar, picks a page from the numbered strip or makes one with the single filled "New page" control, writes with "/", selection and Ctrl K, and never presses save: the stamp shows Saved with the time.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 64px viridian rail. 56px command bar: "Jotbook /", a notebook picker, "/", a section picker, then B/I/U, checklist, Insert, Ctrl K search, the Saved stamp and filled "New page". A 240px page strip of 42px/800 numerals with titles and mono dates. The note sheet (up to 880px) on the 72px grid canvas with a 40px title, a date line, then the body. A 32px status bar with counts and dates.

FORM: Focus sheet, #6 on the ordered structural list for this surface. seed: 8bd946baa065cb28. Signature interaction: the numbered page strip, live, with the current page's numeral in viridian; the strip turns into cross-notebook search results in place. Motion moves on one axis, 160 to 200ms, ease-out.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
