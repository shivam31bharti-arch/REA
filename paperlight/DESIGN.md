# Design System: Paperlight

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Gridded Broadsheet"**

Paperlight is International Typographic Style applied to an office suite. Every screen sits on a visible modular grid; structure is carried by scale (oversized numerals and grotesk headlines), by 1px ink hairlines, and by flat full-height colour fields that tell you which app you are in. There is no icon ribbon, no white page floating on a grey shadowed void, no rounded card. The page is a 794px A4 sheet with a 1px ink edge, set on a canvas ruled every 72px.

The density is that of a calm working tool: a 64px colour rail, a 56px command bar, a 264px outline column and a 32px status bar frame the document, and almost everything else hides in selection, "/" and Ctrl K. Colour is rare and loud: one app field per app, the accent repeated only where it means "you are here" or "this needs you". Everything else is white ground (or graphite in dark) and #111 ink. Screens must survive a compressed screen recording on a phone, which is why outline numerals, field colours and stamp text all clear legibility floors rather than sitting at whisper contrast.

Motion is one axis, short and exponential: things drop 4px into place or rise 6px, never bounce, never scale.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Visible grid: 72px column rules on the editor canvas, 12 columns with 24px gutters on Home and the Dashboard, drawn on both edges of each column; in Cells the spreadsheet's own 30px cell rules are the grid.
- Square everything (0px radius) and 1px ink hairlines in place of shadows.
- One flat colour field per app (Prose vermilion, Cells cobalt, Deck sun, Jotbook viridian), full height in the rail.
- Geist grotesk at extreme scale contrast: 96px/800 display against 13px/500 labels.
- Ordered work is numbered: Prose sections, Jotbook pages, Cells sheets and Deck slides carry 42px/800 numerals with their length beside them (a 4px bar in the outline and strips, the block's own width in the Deck timeline), and the current one turns to the app's accent text.
- Document state is stamped in mono, always visible, never a toast.
- Light and graphite dark themes share the same fields; print and PDF are always light.

## Colors

A white-and-ink Swiss palette with four flat, saturated app fields, each tuned until its on-colour clears 4.5:1.

### Primary
- **Prose Vermilion** (#D63A22): the Prose rail, the Prose field on Home, the selected palette and "/" item, pressed bubble buttons, the caret, checked task boxes, selection tint (24%) and table-cell selection tint (14%). Darkened from the contract's #E8432C because white text on #E8432C is about 4.0:1; on #D63A22 it is about 4.7:1.
- **Vermilion Text** (#C2311A light / #F26B54 dark): vermilion used *as text* on ground: links, the app name in the command-bar crumb, the Unsaved stamp, active menu labels, the current outline numeral, the active sub-heading in the outline, pressed status toggles. Never use the field colour itself for small text or for the current numeral.

### Secondary
- **Cells Cobalt** (#2448D8): Cells' field, rail and accent, with a white on-colour (about 7.0:1). Inside Cells it fills only what means "selected" or "on": the range tint and the selected-header tint (both 14%), a fully selected column or row header (solid, white text), an active filter button, checked filter boxes, the caret, the selected palette item. On Home it is the Cells field, the 28px head band of sheet thumbnails, their hover border and the 10px shelf swatch of Cells drafts and .xlsx recents.
- **Cobalt Text** (#2448D8 light / #7D97FF dark): Cells' text variant, the value of the accent-text role inside Cells: the "Cells" crumb, the current sheet numeral, the selected header's letter or number, the 2px selection frame, the fill handle and fill preview, the copy marquee, the in-cell editor frame, the formula-line focus ring, the column-resize line, the selected function name in suggestions, the selected chart's outline and resize handle, the Unsaved stamp and the "Showing N of M rows" status line. On light it equals the field (#2448D8 clears 7:1 on white); on graphite it lifts to #7D97FF (about 6.6:1 on Graphite Page) so frames and the current numeral stay brighter than Numeral Grey.
- **Sun** (#F2C230): Deck's field and rail, the only field with an ink on-colour (#111, about 11.3:1). Inside Deck it fills only what means "selected" or "now": the object chip that opens the object tools, an 18% tint under the current timeline block, and the current segment of the presenting band. On Home it is the Deck field and the 10px shelf swatch of recent decks. It also lends find-in-document hits their highlight at 55% mix. Sun is never type or a 1px line on white (about 1.7:1); those take Deck Text.

### Named Rules
**The One Field Per App Rule.** Each app owns exactly one flat colour field and it fills the full-height rail. The current app's field is the accent; Home's and the Dashboard's accent is Suite Ink. Field colours are never gradients, tints or decoration.

**The Text-Weight Accent Rule.** A field colour used as text, or as a thin line that must read like text, switches to its app's text variant, the accent-text role: Vermilion Text (#C2311A light, #F26B54 dark) in Prose, Viridian Text (#0B7A6C light, #3FBFA9 dark) in Jotbook, Cobalt Text (#2448D8 light, #7D97FF dark) in Cells, Deck Text (#8C6A00 light, #F2C230 dark) in Deck. The current numeral in the Prose outline, the Jotbook page strip, the Cells sheet strip and the Deck timeline and sorter always takes the text variant, never the field colour, and so do the Cells selection frame, fill handle, editor frame and copy marquee and the Deck insertion marks and template hover edge. Sun shows itself only as a fill (the chip, the current-block tint, the band); its text variant carries everything thin. Any new field colour must clear 4.5:1 against its on-colour before it ships, and any new app defines its text variant alongside it; Deck takes #111 ink, the others white.

**The Slides Are Content Rule.** Slide themes are the deck's own palette, not chrome: they do not follow the light or graphite UI theme, and a slide looks the same on the canvas, in the sorter, when presenting and in .pptx and PDF. Inside a slide, type set in the accent resolves to the theme's accent text, and only the theme's colours or a fixed colour the user picked paint the slide.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. There are no drop shadows anywhere in the build. Depth is conveyed by a 1px Seam border around any plane that floats (menus, the right-click menu, command palette, "/" menu, find bar, link popover, function suggestions, the filter popover, chart frames, dialogs), by ink inversion for transient tools (bubble toolbar, tooltips, chart tooltips, toasts), and by a 28% black scrim behind the palette and dialogs. Rail hover and current states darken the field with black at 14% and 24%, and lighten Suite Ink with white at 10% and 18%. In the grid, layers stack by order alone: range tint, then the selection frame and fill handle, then charts, then the in-cell editor and suggestions. On the slide canvas the same holds: slide content, then the text box being edited, then outlines and handles, then snap guides. The Deck sorter is an opaque ruled plane over the work area, not a floating layer, and presenting replaces the whole window with the black stage; its HUD is an ink chip, the presenting form of ink inversion.

### Named Rules
**The Hairlines Instead Of Shadows Rule.** A floating layer is a flat ground plane with a 1px ink border. If it needs more separation, invert it to ink; never add a shadow or blur.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** draw the grid: 72px column rules on work canvases, 12 columns with 24px gutters on overview screens, lines on both column edges.
- **Do** give every new app (Cells, Deck, Jotbook) its own field colour in the rail and reuse every other token unchanged.
- **Do** check every field colour against its on-colour at 4.5:1 or better, and use the app's text variant for accent text: #C2311A / #F26B54 in Prose, #0B7A6C / #3FBFA9 in Jotbook, #2448D8 / #7D97FF in Cells, #8C6A00 / #F2C230 in Deck.
- **Do** set the current numeral in the outline, the strips, the timeline and the sorter in accent text, and keep inactive numerals at Numeral Grey (#8F8F8C light, #6A6A6A dark) or darker so they read at 3:1 after compression.
- **Do** keep exactly one filled ink control per command bar, and keep every command reachable from Ctrl K when the bar sheds controls.
- **Do** stamp document state (Saved, Unsaved, Exported, with time) in the mono stamp, always visible; autosaving apps seed the time from the last edit.
- **Do** route every delete that can't be undone (Jotbook pages, sections and notebooks; Cells sheets) through a confirm dialog: Cancel first and focused, a spacer, the danger text button last; deletes that Ctrl Z restores at once (Cells rows, columns, charts, cell contents) run straight away with no ellipsis.
- **Do** move on one axis at 160 or 200ms with cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), and drop all motion under reduced motion (the grid glide, the copy marquee, chart grow-in, the object tools' rise, the timeline resize, the sorter fade, the presenting cross-fade and cursor, and the Dashboard's stage fade, band colour and arrow nudge included); the grid's selection frame and fill handle are the one faster step, gliding at 120ms on the same curve.
- **Do** set every figure in the grid, the status bar, the strips, the timeline and on slides with tabular numerals.
- **Do** render print and PDF in light tokens on A4 with 25.4mm margins, whatever the theme; Deck prints one slide per 1280 by 720 page in its slide theme.
- **Do** size Deck's timeline blocks and presenting segments by planned seconds, so the shape of the talk is visible while it is built and while it is given.
- **Do** dim every top-level block except the current one to 0.2 opacity in Focus mode.
- **Do** keep the Dashboard's app strips on the latest work, not on the stage: pointing at an item changes only the stage, so nothing moves under the pointer.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add drop shadows, blur or glows to any layer; use a 1px ink border or ink inversion.
- **Don't** round corners on any element, chart bars and tooltips included.
- **Don't** use field colours as gradients, tints behind text, or decoration outside rails, fields, selection, selected items, search marks and the 10px identity swatches and bands that say which app a piece of work belongs to; current numerals use the text variant, not the field. Deck's 18% Sun tint marks only the current timeline block.
- **Don't** set worded labels in Geist Mono.
- **Don't** place uppercase tracked labels above headings.
- **Don't** put Delete or Discard next to a commit action without space between them.
- **Don't** fill a destructive button or menu item with Danger; danger is text weight only.
- **Don't** let the UI theme or chrome tokens repaint a slide; slides keep their deck theme everywhere, exports included.
